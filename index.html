About

I'm Md. Mominul Islam, a geographer and geospatial analyst from Sylhet, Bangladesh. I'm finishing an MS in Geography and Environment at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (expected December 2026). My thesis reads the Teesta River Basin's seasonal water balance through satellite data and explainable machine learning.

I work as a GIS Analyst (Water Modelling) at SPEKTER GmbH in Germany, supporting hydrological and hydraulic modelling with the MIKE suite and HEC-RAS. My research covers flood susceptibility, earthquake risk, hydroclimatic extremes, wetlands and climate-driven migration, with a spatial model at the centre and a plain question behind it: who is exposed, and why.

I'm looking for a PhD on compound climate risk, flood modelling and machine-learning-based hazard assessment.

FloodML-Geo, a QGIS plugin

Flood susceptibility mapping inside QGIS with explainable machine learning. The flood raster goes in; spatially validated models, SHAP explanations and susceptibility maps come out. Includes a self-test and a check that flags test data overlapping the training data.
