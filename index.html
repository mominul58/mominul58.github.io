About

MS student in Geography and Environment (expected December 2026) at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, specializing in GIS, remote sensing and explainable machine learning for hydroclimatic and multi-hazard risk assessment. Author of 8 published peer-reviewed articles and co-author on 17 additional manuscripts under review at journals including Springer Nature and Taylor & Francis. Combines hands-on hydrological and hydraulic modelling experience (MIKE suite, HEC-RAS) with a strong, active publication record. Seeking a PhD to advance research on compound climate risk, flood modelling and machine-learning-based hazard assessment.

Currently working as a GIS Analyst (Water Modelling) at SPEKTER GmbH, Herzogenaurach, Germany. My work has a spatial model at the centre and a plain question behind it: who is exposed, and why.

FloodML-Geo, a QGIS plugin

Flood susceptibility mapping inside QGIS with explainable machine learning. The flood raster goes in; spatially validated models, SHAP explanations and susceptibility maps come out. Includes a self-test and a check that flags test data overlapping the training data.
